About ARM

The Association Des Ressortissants de Mamou (ARM) unites people from Mamou, Guinea, and their families living abroad. We stay connected, celebrate our culture, and work together on projects that make a lasting difference back home.

Official registration gives the association an accurate member list so we can communicate clearly, organize effectively, and represent our community with one voice.

Why register

  • Official membershipReceive your ARM member ID.
  • Community tiesStay connected through events and mutual support.
  • Development projectsHelp coordinate projects that improve life in Mamou.
  • Member benefitsAccess member announcements and programs.

A federation of Mamou's sub-prefectures

ARM is run as a federal system. The associations of the sub-prefectures of Mamou and Timbo each work locally, united under ARM, and ARM partners with the Dalaba and Pita associations across the Mamou region.
