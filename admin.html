Who receives texts?
  1. Only Active members whose Texts column is Yes. Members choose this when they register.
  2. For members who registered earlier: once a member agrees (in writing, e.g. a text or a sign-up sheet), set their Texts column to Yes in the ARM Members spreadsheet.
  3. A member who replies STOP is unsubscribed by Twilio; their Texts column is set to No at the next send.
  4. Send texts between 9 am and 8 pm. Delivery problems after a text is accepted appear in the Twilio Console → Monitor → Messaging logs.

How to download transactions from Chase
  1. Sign in at chase.com and open the account.
  2. Click the download icon above the transaction list ("Download account activity").
  3. Choose file type CSV and the date range, then Download.
  4. Click "Import Chase CSV" here and pick the file. Transactions already imported are skipped automatically.
