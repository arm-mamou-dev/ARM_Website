Become a member in three steps

Registration is free. Your member ID is the key to your card, your status and the projects you support.

Register as a member
  1. 01

    Register online

    Fill in your name, phone, state and sub-prefecture of origin. It takes about a minute.

  2. 02

    Receive your member card

    You get your member ID right away, with a card showing your prefecture, district and state.

  3. 03

    Take part

    Leadership confirms your membership. Then join meetings, support projects and follow our progress.
