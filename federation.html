The ARM federation

ARM is run as a federal system: the associations of Mamou's 14 communes and sub-prefectures are united under one federation.

  1. ARM Federal level: leadership, shared projects and the member register
  2. 14 sub-prefecture associations Local level: each community organizes its own members and activities
  3. Members Every member registers once with ARM and belongs to their sub-prefecture
