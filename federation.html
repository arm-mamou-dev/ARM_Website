The ARM federation

The Mamou region has four prefectures: Mamou, Timbo, Dalaba and Pita, with 36 sub-prefectures. ARM unites the associations of Mamou and Timbo, and works as a partner with the Dalaba and Pita associations in the USA.

  1. ARM Federal level: leadership, shared projects and the member register
  2. Sub-prefecture associations Local level: each community organizes its own members and activities
  3. Members Every member registers once with ARM and belongs to their sub-prefecture
